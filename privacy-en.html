Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

Learning Islands (the "App") is made by Dao Vui Kids ("we") to help young children learn letters, numbers, colours, shapes and animals. This policy explains how the App handles information. It is written for parents and guardians.

2. Summary

  • We do NOT collect personal information from children or parents.
  • The App has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The App shows no ads and uses no third-party tracking or analytics tools.
  • All learning data stays on your device.

3. Data stored on the device

So your child can carry on where they left off, the App stores on the device itself:

  • Learning progress: lessons and levels finished, stars.
  • In-game rewards: coins, diamonds, clothes and decorations bought in the Baby House.
  • The parent report: lessons finished and time spent in the App per day (kept for up to 60 days).
  • Drawings your child saves in the App.
  • Settings: language, music, sound.
  • A technical error log: up to the last 200 errors the App ran into (the error and where in the code), with no personal information.

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete all of it by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in the device settings.

4. Backups

  • On Android, if Google backup is turned on for the device, Android may back up the App's progress data (not drawings) to your Google account so it comes back after reinstalling or on a new phone. Google keeps that backup in your account; we cannot access it.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the App's data may be part of the device's iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings.

5. Membership purchases

Memberships are bought through Google Play or the App Store, and payments are handled by Google or Apple under their own policies. We do not receive or store your payment details, card or account name. The App only receives from the store which plan was bought and whether it is still active, to unlock content.

6. Sharing drawings and sending bug reports

The share button for drawings is behind the parental gate. When you choose to share a drawing, the App opens your device's share sheet so you can pick where to send it. The App never sends drawings anywhere by itself.

In the Parent Corner, "Report a bug / Feedback" opens your email app with a ready-made message to us containing the App version, the operating system and the error log. You can see all of it and decide whether to send it. We only use it to fix problems and reply to you.

7. Permissions

The App does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or your photo library. It only uses the network to talk to Google Play or the App Store when buying or restoring a plan.

8. Children's privacy

  • The App is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families and App Store Kids policies.
  • Purchases, the Parent Corner and sharing drawings are protected by a parental gate (a question young children cannot yet answer).
  • There are no ads, no links to outside websites in the children's area, and no chat or contact with strangers.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Third parties

The App only works with the in-app purchase services of Google Play or the App Store. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the effective date at the top and publish the new version in the App and on the App's page. If a change affects data collection, we will say so clearly before it applies.

11. Contact
